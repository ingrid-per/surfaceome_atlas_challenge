Raised across many cancer types

breadth, not score
Ordered by the number of cancer types the gene passes in, then by its median S across them. This is the one list that does not rank on S — a gene can lead it without being the most specific anywhere.
Treat this list with particular caution. Immune infiltration and stromal content are present in tumours of every kind, so ranking by breadth tends to surface genes carried by those cells rather than by tumour cells — several entries here are known immune or fibroblast proteins. Tumour purity is not accounted for anywhere in this analysis, and it bites hardest on this tab.
